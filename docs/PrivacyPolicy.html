Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MindWeaver is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services.

1.1 User-Generated Content

  • Predictions: When you accept or deny AI predictions, your responses are stored locally on your device.
  • Dream Records: Text descriptions of your dreams that you manually enter are stored locally.
  • Journal Entries: Your reflection journal entries are stored locally on your device.
  • Quiz Results: Personality quiz responses and analysis results are stored locally.

1.2 Usage Data

  • App Usage: We track basic app usage (opens, features used) locally on your device to improve user experience.
  • Points & Achievements: Your points, streaks, and achievement progress are stored locally.

1.3 Device Data

  • Device Token: If you enable push notifications, a device token is collected for notification delivery purposes.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash logs may be collected to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed as follows:

  • On-Device AI: All AI predictions and dream analysis are performed ON-DEVICE using Apple's CoreML framework. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Local Storage: All user content (predictions, dreams, journals) is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Aggregate, anonymized usage data may be used to improve the app experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Local-Only Storage: All sensitive user data is stored locally on your device, not on external servers.
  • On-Device Processing: AI processing happens entirely on your device. Your thoughts, dreams, and reflections are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Secure Deletion: You can delete all your data by uninstalling the app. All local data will be permanently removed.
  • No Account Required: MindWeaver does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

4. AI Technology Disclosure

MindWeaver uses advanced AI technologies:

  • On-Device Machine Learning: All AI predictions and analysis are powered by Apple's CoreML framework running entirely on your device.
  • No Cloud AI Processing: We do not send your data to external AI services or cloud-based APIs for processing.
  • Prediction Algorithm: The AI generates random thought predictions to create engaging user experiences. Predictions are not based on personal data analysis.
  • Dream Analysis: Dream interpretations are generated using on-device machine learning models. No dream content is stored on external servers.

5. In-App Purchases

MindWeaver offers premium subscriptions through the App Store:

  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We never collect your payment information.
  • Purchase History: Your subscription status is managed by Apple and stored locally on your device.
  • Restoration: You can restore purchases using the "Restore Purchase" feature in the app.

6. Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications:

  • Notification Content: We may send you notifications about daily challenges, streak reminders, and achievement unlocks.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or within the app.
  • No Profiling: Notifications are not used for advertising or user profiling.

7. Children's Privacy

MindWeaver is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can view all data stored locally by opening the app.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data.
  • Export: You can export your prediction history from the Profile section.
  • Privacy Controls: You can manage all privacy settings within the app's Settings screen.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
